Glossary

Event F1 (time only)
Combines sensitivity and precision (their harmonic mean): 1 means every reference artifact was found and nothing extra was marked; 0 means no artifact was found.
Sensitivity: proportion of reference events found
The proportion of the reference's artifacts that the annotator found.
Precision: proportion of your events that match the reference
The proportion of the annotator's annotations that match an artifact in the reference.
Cohen's kappa over time (exact)
Agreement, moment by moment, on whether an artifact is present, corrected for the agreement expected by chance: 1 is perfect agreement, 0 is no better than chance.
Positive agreement over time (exact)
Agreement on the time marked as artifact: twice the time both marked, divided by the time each marked. Clean time that both left unmarked does not count.
Positive agreement over time and channels
Positive agreement counted separately for every channel, so choosing the wrong channels lowers it.
Mean temporal overlap (IoU) of matched events
For artifacts that were found: the time both marked divided by the time either marked (intersection over union), averaged. 1 means identical start and end.
Mean channel overlap (Jaccard) of matched events
For artifacts that were found: the channels both chose divided by the channels either chose, averaged (a whole-montage annotation counts as every channel).
Same label as the reference for detected artifacts
The proportion of found artifacts that were given the same artifact type as in the reference.
Extra events (matching no reference artifact) per minute
Annotations that match no reference artifact of any type, per minute of recording (lower is better). An artifact found with a different type counts under 'Same label' instead.
